# ADR 0006: A proposed w3id.org namespace

Status: Proposed

## Context

Issue 13 asked for a persistent identifier.

## Decision

Use https://w3id.org/dcat-uk-ap/ for the profile, its terms and codelists.

## Consequences

Identifiers do not resolve until the owning team registers the redirect.
