# ADR 0008: Cross-border flows as two properties and a rule

Status: Accepted

## Context

Data moves to the Crown Dependencies, Overseas Territories, Commonwealth and partners. UK GDPR restricts personal data leaving the UK; the ICO updated its guidance on 15 January 2026.

## Decision

Record every place the data is held or sent, and the transfer safeguard. Rule R-08 requires the safeguard when personal data leaves the UK. The jurisdiction list is open, in ISO 3166 form.

## Consequences

The record states the basis; it is not legal advice and not a transfer risk assessment. The adequacy column must be kept current (O-15).
