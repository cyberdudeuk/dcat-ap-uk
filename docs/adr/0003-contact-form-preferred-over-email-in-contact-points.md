# ADR 0003: Contact form preferred over email in contact points

Status: Accepted

## Context

Issue 71 found the contact point was defined as an email address. Email addresses go stale when people move, and can be personal data.

## Decision

A contact point gives a contact form, a team mailbox or both, and never a named person. The form is preferred. The portal publishes no email address at all.

## Consequences

Existing records with team mailboxes still conform. Records with personal addresses should be changed.
