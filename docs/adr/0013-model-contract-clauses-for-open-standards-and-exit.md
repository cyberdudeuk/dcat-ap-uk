# ADR 0013: Model contract clauses for open standards and exit

Status: Proposed

## Context

Lock-in comes from proprietary formats, closed interfaces and missing exit terms. The Open Standards Principles and the Technology Code of Practice ask buyers to prevent it.

## Decision

Offer clauses in seven groups (open standards, evidence, ownership, exit, change and debt, security, accessibility) as a downloadable file.

## Consequences

They are a starting point, not legal advice. Commercial and legal review comes first (O-20).
