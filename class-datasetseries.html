    Dataset series

    Updated 8 sections on this page are new or updated since 1.2.0-rc.1 (2026-11-06). Everything that changed

    A group of datasets published separately but sharing a subject and a method, such as each release of a census.

    Mandatory properties in detailUpdated

    Title dct:title

    The name of the resource, in plain English.

    Obligation
    Mandatory
    Cardinality
    1..n
    IRI
    dct:title
    Value
    text
    Why it matters
    Helps people find and recognise the resource in search results.
    Not to be confused with
    Alternative title (a shorter or informal name) and identifier (a code, not a name).
    How to fill it in
    Use sentence case. Include the topic and, where it helps, the place and period. Avoid acronyms and jargon, or spell them out. Give a Welsh title too where the publisher has Welsh language duties, tagged with the language.
    Example
    Postcode locations for Great Britain
    Since the xGov MEM
    Unchanged
    Also known as
    2026 guidance: title; Data Marketplace: title; schema.org: name K
    Backlog
    #76

    Description dct:description

    A plain English account of what the resource contains and what it can be used for.

    Obligation
    Mandatory
    Cardinality
    1..n
    IRI
    dct:description
    Value
    text
    Why it matters
    Lets people decide whether the resource answers their question before they ask for it.
    Not to be confused with
    Abstract (a one-sentence summary for search results) and keyword (single words or phrases).
    How to fill it in
    Say what the data is about, who or what it covers, how it was collected and what it is good for. Put warnings about use in the access rights or the licence, not here.
    Example
    The location of every postcode unit in Great Britain, as a single point, with the local authority and ward it falls in.
    Since the xGov MEM
    Unchanged
    Also known as
    2026 guidance: description; Data Marketplace: description; schema.org: description K

    Recommended properties in detailUpdated

    Publisher dct:publisher

    The organisation responsible for making the resource available.

    Obligation
    Recommended
    Cardinality
    0..1
    IRI
    dct:publisher
    Value
    object: Organisation, from GOV.UK organisations
    Why it matters
    Says who is accountable for the resource being available, and to whom questions about access go.
    Not to be confused with
    Creator (who made the content) and contact point (where to send questions).
    How to fill it in
    Use the organisation's GOV.UK organisation address (for example https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/ordnance-survey), or the equivalent register entry for a body that is not on GOV.UK. Never name a person.
    Example
    https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/ordnance-survey
    Since the xGov MEM
    Range changed. Was a GOV.UK organisation slug from a 2023 copy of the list (1,176 entries). Now an agent identified by its GOV.UK organisation address, from the list read on 31 October 2026 (1,271 entries, closed organisations kept and flagged).
    Also known as
    Data Marketplace: publisher; schema.org: publisher K
    Backlog
    #69

    Contact point dcat:contactPoint

    Where to ask questions about the resource or give feedback on it.

    Obligation
    Recommended
    Cardinality
    0..n
    IRI
    dcat:contactPoint
    Value
    object: Contact point
    Why it matters
    Gives people a route to the team that can answer questions about the resource.
    Not to be confused with
    Publisher (an organisation) and the contact point's own properties (form, email, telephone).
    How to fill it in
    Give a team, never a named person. A contact form is the preferred route: it keeps addresses private and can be moved when teams change. An address for a shared team mailbox is allowed; a personal address is not.
    Since the xGov MEM
    Definition fixed. The definition was a copy of the email definition (issue 71). It now describes the contact point as a whole.
    Also known as
    Data Marketplace: contactPoint; schema.org: contactPoint (via publisher) K
    Backlog
    #71 #15

    Update frequency dct:accrualPeriodicity

    How often the resource is updated.

    Obligation
    Recommended
    Cardinality
    0..1
    IRI
    dct:accrualPeriodicity
    Value
    concept, from Update frequency
    Why it matters
    Lets people plan when to come back, and harvesters plan when to check for changes.
    How to fill it in
    Choose from the EU frequency list that DCAT-AP uses. If it is not updated on a schedule, choose irregular; if it will never be updated, choose never.
    Example
    eufreq:QUARTERLY
    Since the xGov MEM
    Codelist mapped (was updateFrequency). Dublin Core frequency terms are mapped one to one to the EU frequency vocabulary that DCAT-AP requires. Old values are still accepted and converted.
    Also known as
    Data Marketplace: updateFrequency

    Optional properties in detailUpdated

    Geographic coverage dct:spatial

    The geographic area the data is about.

    Obligation
    Optional
    Cardinality
    0..n
    IRI
    dct:spatial
    Value
    object: Location
    Why it matters
    Lets people find data for a place, and see at a glance whether it covers the area they need.
    How to fill it in
    Prefer the identifier of a standard area: a GSS code from the ONS (for example K02000001 for the United Kingdom) for statistical and administrative areas, or another controlled list such as national parks. Use a bounding box or a geometry only where no named area fits. Say whether the data covers the whole area or only some places in it (issue 1). For geospatial data, also follow UK GEMINI.
    Example
    http://statistics.data.gov.uk/id/statistical-geography/K03000001
    Since the xGov MEM
    New
    Also known as
    schema.org: spatialCoverage K
    Backlog
    #1

    Time period covered dct:temporal

    The period of time the data is about.

    Obligation
    Optional
    Cardinality
    0..n
    IRI
    dct:temporal
    Value
    object: Period of time
    Why it matters
    Lets people find data for the years they need. It is not the date the data was published.
    How to fill it in
    Give a start date, an end date, or both. An open-ended period has only a start date. Each census is its own dataset in a series.
    Since the xGov MEM
    New
    Also known as
    2026 guidance: temporalCoverage; schema.org: temporalCoverage K
    Backlog
    #2

    Theme dcat:theme

    The broad subject of the resource, from a controlled list.

    Obligation
    Optional
    Cardinality
    0..n
    IRI
    dcat:theme
    Value
    concept, from Theme
    Why it matters
    Lets people browse by subject, and catalogues group the same subjects across publishers.
    Not to be confused with
    Keyword, which is free text.
    How to fill it in
    Choose one or more themes from the DCAT-AP-UK theme list, which uses the data.gov.uk topics. Each is mapped to the EU data themes, so records also make sense to European tools. The cross-government taxonomy from the master and reference data working group will replace or extend it when it is published (issue 49).
    Example
    ukcl:theme/mapping
    Since the xGov MEM
    Codelist added. The xGov MEM had no list of values (issue 49). The 14 data.gov.uk topics, read on 6 November 2026, are now the list.
    Also known as
    Data Marketplace: theme; schema.org: about K
    Backlog
    #49

    Last updated dct:modified

    The date the content of the resource last changed.

    Obligation
    Optional
    Cardinality
    0..1
    IRI
    dct:modified
    Value
    date
    Why it matters
    Helps people judge whether the data is current enough for their use.
    Not to be confused with
    The date the metadata record changed, which belongs on the catalogue record.
    How to fill it in
    Give it whenever you know it. If you do not know it, leave it out rather than using the date the record was harvested: a false date is worse than none (issue 30).
    Example
    2026-08-01
    Since the xGov MEM
    Obligation changed. Mandatory in the xGov MEM, but many sources do not provide it (issue 30). Recommended now, and never to be invented.
    Also known as
    schema.org: dateModified K
    Backlog
    #30
